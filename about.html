About the Club

Research beyond the classroom.

The Research Club gives EAHS students space to investigate questions, develop independent academic work, and share what they learn.

Our Purpose

Students often find questions worth exploring that do not fit neatly into a school assignment. The Research Club provides a place to work through those questions with other students, discuss methods and sources, and receive feedback along the way.

Members can begin with a new idea or continue a project they've already started. Individual and collaborative projects are both welcome.

What Counts as Research?

Science & Mathematics

Experiments, quantitative analysis, literature reviews, modeling, and other scientific investigations.

Technology & Engineering

Software, engineering designs, prototypes, simulations, and technical documentation.

Humanities & Social Sciences

Historical research, surveys, literary analysis, and other evidence-based inquiry.

How It Works

Bring a topic, research question, or work in progress. Meetings are meant for discussing ideas, sharing sources, making progress, and learning from others. Members who complete work they are comfortable sharing may have it added to our research archive.

Publishing isn't a membership requirement. The club exists to support the process of learning and investigation, not to impose an artificial deadline on every project.

Club Organization

The club has school approval and is in its founding stage. A faculty adviser and meeting arrangements are being finalized. Leadership and meeting details will be added once formally confirmed.
