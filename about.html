Easton Area High School Research Club
About Us

Our Mission

Making independent research more accessible to students across Easton Area High School.

Research Beyond the Classroom

The club supports students who want to explore subjects in greater depth, whether they have a detailed research proposal or are just beginning to think about a question. Members can discuss sources, experimental methods, designs, data, and conclusions together.

Projects may be individual or collaborative. The emphasis is on genuine academic inquiry rather than a single required format or weekly assignment.

What Can Members Research?

Science & Mathematics

Experiments, quantitative studies, literature reviews, and mathematical investigations.

Engineering & Technology

Prototypes, computer science, simulations, engineering designs, and technical documentation.

Humanities & Social Sciences

Historical research, social inquiry, analytical essays, and interdisciplinary questions.

How the Club Works

  • Begin. Bring a topic, existing project, or question you want to explore.
  • Develop. Investigate sources, discuss your approach, and exchange feedback.
  • Share. Completed public work may be added to the club's Research Archive.

Founding Organization

The club has received school approval and is currently completing the steps needed to begin holding meetings. The faculty adviser, meeting schedule, and formal board details have not yet been announced.

Students who are interested in joining can review the Membership page for the latest information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need research experience to join?

No. Students with any experience level are welcome, including students who want to learn how to begin a project.

Can members work together?

Yes. Students may choose independent or collaborative work.

Is publication required?

No. The archive is an option for completed public work, not a membership requirement.
