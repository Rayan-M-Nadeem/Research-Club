EAHS Research Club / About

A place for curiosity to become real work.

The EAHS Research Club supports student-directed academic projects outside the boundaries of a typical class assignment.

Our purpose

Research begins with a question.

Members can develop an original project, explore a field they want to understand, continue independent work, or collaborate with classmates. The goal is to make research more approachable and give students a community where their work can grow.

Projects can include literature reviews, experiments, engineering designs, data analysis, historical studies, computational work, and other meaningful investigations.

How the club works

From first idea to final project.

STEP 01

Start with an idea

Bring a research question, an area of interest, a problem to solve, or an existing project.

STEP 02

Develop your work

Discuss methods, evaluate sources, collaborate, share progress, and get feedback.

STEP 03

Share your findings

Completed work may be featured in the club's public research archive when ready for release.

Who can participate?

EAHS students interested in academic inquiry, with or without prior research experience.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need prior research experience?

No. Students can learn research methods while developing their first project.

Can I work with classmates?

Yes. The club supports individual and collaborative research.

Do I have to publish something?

No. Publishing is an opportunity, not a requirement for membership.
