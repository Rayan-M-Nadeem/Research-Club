About

What the club is for.

EAHS Research Club is built around student-directed academic work and gives members a place to develop projects outside normal coursework.

Purpose

Work that does not fit neatly into a class.

The club gives students a place to develop a new project, continue existing work, discuss methods, work through sources, and get feedback before something is finished.

How It Works

Start anywhere

You do not need a finished research question. A subject, problem, or general area of interest is enough to begin.

Choose the format

Projects can be papers, literature reviews, experiments, engineering designs, simulations, data analysis, historical studies, surveys, or other research-based work.

Use meetings

Meetings can be used for project discussion, source review, feedback, presentations, or help working through a specific problem.

Release the work

Completed public work can be added to the club's Research page so it remains easy to find after a project is finished.

Board

Club board.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need research experience?

No. The club can be used to learn the process from the beginning.

Do I need a project before joining?

No. You can join with an existing project, a possible topic, or just an area you want to explore.

Can I work with someone else?

Yes. Individual and collaborative projects can both fit the club.
