About Us

Our Mission

Research should not be limited to class assignments. Our goal is to give EAHS students the chance to pursue questions that matter to them, exchange ideas, and build thoughtful academic projects at their own pace.

Students can explore science, mathematics, engineering, technology, the humanities, social sciences, and more.
