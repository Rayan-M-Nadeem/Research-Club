What To Expect

Bring an idea, or find one here.

The club is not designed around mandatory weekly deliverables. Members can use meetings to make steady progress, get feedback, and work through whatever stage of a project they are currently in.

Before Joining

Do I have to publish something?

No. Publication can be a goal, but it is not a requirement for membership.

Can I join just to learn?

Yes. Students can use the club to learn how research works before committing to a larger project.

Can I work in any subject?

Yes, as long as the project has a genuine research or investigative component.
