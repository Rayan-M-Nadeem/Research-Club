Interested in science, engineering, writing, history, mathematics, or something else? You don't need a finished project to be part of the club.

Membership

Everyone starts somewhere.

The club welcomes Easton Area High School students who want to try independent research or continue an existing project. Beginners are encouraged to participate.

Who can join?

EAHS students interested in research or independent academic work.

Experience needed

None. Bring an idea, a question, or curiosity.

What to expect

Make research your own.

The club is built for steady exploration and student ownership of projects, not mandatory weekly assignments.

Get started

Bring your curiosity

You can join with a topic, an existing project, or no research question at all.

Work together

Share ideas & feedback

Build something

Create at your pace

Develop a paper, experiment, design, presentation, or other investigative project.

Common questions

Before you join.

Is research publication required?

No. Publication is optional, and projects can develop at different speeds.

Can I work with friends?

Yes. Individual and collaborative projects are welcome.

Which subjects are allowed?

Science, social science, mathematics, technology, the humanities, and more—as long as the work has a genuine investigative component.
