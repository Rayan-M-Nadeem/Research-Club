Easton Area High School Research Club
Get Involved

Join the Research Club

Whether you have an existing project or are starting from scratch, the club is open to students interested in independent academic work.

Who Can Join?

EAHS students who are curious about research are welcome. You do not need prior research experience, a finished project, or a specific subject in mind.

Students may explore topics independently, join collaborative projects, and participate in discussions or presentations.

What to Expect

Members can bring a question, propose a project, or simply learn the basics of doing research. Meetings can provide time to discuss ideas, evaluate sources, exchange feedback, and share progress. There are no mandatory weekly publications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to publish a paper?

No. Publishing is optional, and the club welcomes projects in many different formats.

Can I work with other students?

Yes. Group work and independent work are both welcome.

Can I join without a project?

Yes. You can start with a broad interest and refine your question as you learn.
