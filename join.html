Membership

Get involved.

You don't need prior research experience or a finished project. Bring a question, an idea, or simply an interest in learning.

Who Can Join?

EAHS students interested in research or independent academic work are welcome, including those who have never done a research project before.

You can work alone, collaborate with other members, and use meetings to explore a new subject or make progress on an existing project.

Common Questions

Do I need a project before I join?

No. Students can explore possible questions and develop ideas with the club.

Can I work with friends?

Yes. Individual and collaborative projects are both welcome.

Do I have to publish something?

No. Sharing completed work is an opportunity, not a condition of membership.
